The Slack provider uses Slack's MCP server. To use it, you must set up a Slack app in your Slack workspace.

<Steps titleSize="h3">
  <Step title="Add the Slack provider">
    In your Coral workspace, add the Slack provider. In the Setup step, find the **OAuth Application** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your Slack app">
    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="I already have a Slack app configured">
      Find your Slack app's client ID and client secret in the **Basic Information** section of your [Slack app's settings](https://api.slack.com/apps).
      </Tab>

      <Tab title="I need to create a Slack app">
        1. In Coral, click **Create a Slack app**. A new tab opens at https://api.slack.com/apps?new_app=1, with the app's details already filled in.
        1. Review the app's JSON config. It is the config that [Slack recommends](https://docs.slack.dev/ai/slack-mcp-server/#authentication).
        1. Choose the Slack workspace that you want to add the app to. Then follow Slack's flow. Depending on your permissions, you add the app to your workspace or request approval from an admin.
        1. Note your new Slack app's client ID and client secret. They are in the **Basic Information** section of your Slack app's settings page.

        <Note>You can't finish adding the Slack provider to Coral until the app is in your Slack workspace. If you must wait for an admin to approve it, come back when the app is approved. Then repeat these steps with the "I already have a Slack app configured" tab.</Note>
     </Tab>
    </Tabs>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the setup">
    Enter your Slack app's client ID and client secret in Coral's provider setup screen. Then click **Next** to continue.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Configuring your Slack app manually

If you don't want to use Coral's **Create a Slack app** link, create the app yourself with [Slack's instructions](https://docs.slack.dev/ai/slack-mcp-server/#authentication).
