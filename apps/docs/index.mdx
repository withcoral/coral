![Coral, with engraved orange corals](/images/cover.webp)

Coral is the trusted execution layer for agents. Connect your agents to one MCP
server, and they can call every <Tooltip tip="GitHub, Slack, Notion, and anything else behind an OpenAPI document or an MCP server.">provider</Tooltip> in your workspace.

Coral stores your credentials and adds them to each call, so your agents never
hold them. It checks every call against the policies you set. When a program
works, your agent can save it as a function and reuse it next time.

You run Coral yourself. Your data, credentials and usage history stay on your
machine or your own server.

## Get started

<Steps titleSize="h3">
  <Step title="Install Coral">
    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Desktop app">
        Run Coral on your own machine, for yourself. Download the desktop app
        for macOS, Windows, or Linux from the
        [releases page](https://github.com/withcoral/coral-releases/releases).

        [Install the desktop app](/getting-started/install-locally)
      </Tab>

      <Tab title="Shared server">
        Run Coral as a server your team signs in to, using Coral's official container images.

        [Run a shared server](/getting-started/self-host)
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>

  </Step>

<Step title="Add your providers">
  Add GitHub, Slack, Datadog, and other providers to your workspace. Your agents
  can only call tools from providers you've added.

  [Add a provider](/getting-started/quickstart#add-a-provider)
</Step>

  <Step title="Connect your agents to Coral via MCP">
    Configure your MCP clients (e.g. Claude Code, Codex) to use the [Coral MCP server](/getting-started/quickstart#connect-an-mcp-client)
  </Step>
</Steps>
