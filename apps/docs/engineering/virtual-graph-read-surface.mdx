# Virtual Graph - openCypher Read Surface: Engineering Record

**Status: COMPLETE to its honest boundary: 726 openCypher read scenarios, with
646 supported row-result scenarios and 80 documented-limit error scenarios.**
GraphQL coverage is 164 scenarios, with 145 supported row-result scenarios and
19 validation or error scenarios. Backend: DataFusion 53.

This record consolidates the shipped surface, the deferred boundaries with
their evidence, the ratified design decisions, and the durable gate rules. It
is an internal engineering ledger for future maintainers and agents, not a
user-facing product overview.

## 1. Shipped Surface

- **Staged planning** shipped 8 structural classes: foundation, aggregation,
  direction, scalar alias, multi-hop, optional match, aggregate carry into
  `MATCH`, bare relationship carry into `OPTIONAL MATCH`, plus
  declaration-inferred anonymous endpoints.
- **Temporal support** is product-story complete for naive `date`,
  `localdatetime`, and `localtime`, including flagship warehouse `DATE` stored
  columns; durations, including arithmetic, ISO rendering, `between`,
  components, and unit totals; temporal filters, including GraphQL date ranges
  plus `IN` and `NOT IN`; and zoned datetime Z1-Z3, including full IANA and DST
  support through Arrow `Timestamp(ns, Some(zone))`.
- **UNWIND row sources** shipped 6 dynamic classes alongside static expansion:
  literal no-`MATCH`, variable or alias, non-bare `RETURN`, `collect()`,
  `keys()` and metadata, nested-list typing, plus static-to-dynamic fallthrough
  above the static branch cap.
- **OPTIONAL unanchored support** shipped 3 classes: leading node-only, leading
  relationship pattern, and consecutive multi-optional, using a single-row
  driver `LEFT JOIN` plus multi-branch collapse.
- **Bounded variable-length path expansion** shipped for finite mandatory
  ranges and fixed alternatives, including zero-hop and positive bounded
  ranges. Unbounded recursive traversal remains outside the completed surface.
- **Additional completed features** include `percentileDisc` window emulation,
  BENCH-727 `to*()` `NULL` semantics, fixed-hop path-value materialization for
  `RETURN p` and through-`WITH` carry, and `labels(null)`.

## 2. Ratified Design Decisions

These decisions are James's final position for the completed read surface; they
are not pending design questions.

1. **The typed invariant is the contract.** Labels and relationship types must
   be resolvable. The #545 work extended resolution to unlabeled endpoints that
   are inferable from a unique relationship declaration. That is the boundary's
   final position. There is no anonymous-resolution or schemaless mode unless a
   future explicit opt-in design adds declared union relationships or query
   hints as an additive model. The reasoning is SQL predictability, where a
   query maps to a determinate relational plan, plus the fan-out and ambiguity
   cost of multi-type-union semantics. Ambiguous-inference queries such as
   `(a)-->(b)` across multiple relationship types, unlabeled-first-bound
   queries, and anonymous endpoints that cannot infer a unique label are
   permanently out of scope.
2. **Unbounded variable-length traversal is demand-gated.** Finite bounded
   mandatory ranges are shipped, but recursive `[*]` traversal is the next
   flagship only if product usage shows traversal demand. Recursive expansion
   is a major epic, and the demand-evidence standard gates it.
3. **Small deferrals and TCK negatives are closed permanently.** Path-valued
   pattern comprehension, leading-`WITH`-null row sources, and the TCK negative
   scenarios are closed unless reopened by a product pull.
4. **The headline is that the openCypher read surface is done.** It is complete
   to its honest boundary. Further TCK read yield requires a model opt-in
   design under decision 1 or product-pulled demand under decisions 2 and 3.

## 3. Deferred Boundaries With Evidence

- **Ambiguous typed-model inference** is design-closed by decision 1. The
  shipped model supports declaration-aware inference only when the declaration
  produces one unique endpoint label or relationship type.
- **Unbounded variable-length traversal and variable-length path values** are
  demand-gated by decision 2. The shipped surface includes finite bounded
  mandatory ranges, but unbounded recursive ranges and variable-length graph
  value materialization remain rejected.
- **Path-valued pattern comprehension and leading-`WITH`-null row sources** are
  closed by decision 3.
- **ZonedTime** remains deferred because DataFusion has no timezone-aware
  `TIME` type. Zoned `datetime(...)` is shipped through Arrow
  `Timestamp(ns, Some(zone))`; zoned `time(...)` is not.
- **DST gap and overlap local-time resolution** remains guarded by rejection
  because there is no TCK authority for the intended behavior. The guard is
  revisable only on product demand or new conformance evidence.

## 4. Durable Gate Rules

- **Measure before commit:** count by first blocker on raw reject strings, and
  remeasure before every dispatch. Stale numbers mis-scope work.
- **Probe first:** every DataFusion assumption must be verified before building.
  Scope probes answer actionable versus boundary before anyone writes a fix.
- **Pre-review gate:** the manager runs set-diff, rebase, and additive checks at
  worker completion before reviewer dispatch.
- **Adversarial review:** independent reviewers use multi-label, multi-type,
  and verbatim-repro probes. This caught 3 real correctness bugs before prod on
  the OPTIONAL epic alone.
- **Guard, do not guess:** behavior that cannot be pinned gets a documented
  guard, not a guessed implementation.
- **Multi-branch collapse:** unlabeled and multi-type patterns need multi-label
  or multi-type fixtures. Single-branch coverage hides fan-out bugs.
- **Quality baseline:** determinism is clean at 0 flakies across 22,435
  executions; `perf-check` is tightened to 0.45s, leaving roughly 2.3x
  headroom; `sql_tests` owns SQL-lowering coverage, with e2e deduplicated only
  where TCK coverage supersedes it.
